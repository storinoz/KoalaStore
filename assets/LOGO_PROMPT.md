## Prompt utilizado

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE polished Android app foreground icon for KoalaStore, using the attached logo as the identity reference. Keep the recognizable happy koala head, large rounded ears, closed cheerful curved eyes, oval charcoal nose, and friendly curved smile from the reference. Fill the face and ears in soft medium koala gray, with charcoal clean outlines and lighter gray ear details. Add a small simple emerald-green retail price tag tucked at the lower right of the face, with a white circular hole; this is a subtle sales cue. Flat vector-like illustration, extremely clean and readable at 48px, no gradients, no shadows, no letters or text, no busy background, no frame. Genuine transparent background around the artwork; the face itself is solid gray. Square composition, all artwork centered in the inner 60 percent of the square with generous transparent margins, for Android adaptive icon mask safety. Deliver a single finished icon, no variants or presentation board.

## Prompt final

Use case: logo-brand / precise-object-edit. Edit target: supplied KoalaStore icon. Create a revised square mobile app mark on genuinely transparent background. Keep the emerald green, charcoal outline and silver gray koala identity, friendly rounded flat vector-like style. Change composition substantially: a large emerald green retail price tag with a bold white dollar symbol is the dominant foreground object occupying about 75% of visible area. A small subdued silver-gray koala head, matching supplied face and rounded ears, peeks from behind the upper left of the tag and occupies only about 25% of visible area. The sales symbol must be recognizable first, koala second at 48px. Thick clean outlines, generous surrounding transparent padding suitable for Android adaptive icon. No words, no store name, no slogan, no watermark. No background or square backdrop. Primary focus SALES.
